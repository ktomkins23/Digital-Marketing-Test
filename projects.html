SmartSip

SmartSip is a business idea for a stainless steel water bottle that uses sensors and a connected app to help users track their water intake.

Product

The bottle would be available in three sizes: 20 oz, 32 oz, and 40 oz. The starting price would be approximately $39.99 for the 20 oz version.

Technology

Sensors inside the bottle would track hydration and connect to an app. The app could track water intake and provide recommendations about how much water the user should drink.

Target Customer

SmartSip would primarily target active people and customers who want help staying hydrated throughout the day.

AgTech Degree Proposal
